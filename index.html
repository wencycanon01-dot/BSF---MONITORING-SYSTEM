Operations manual

Black soldier fly rearing and system guide

What is a black soldier fly?

Hermetia illucens is a harmless, non-pest fly. Adults have no functional mouthparts, so they do not bite, do not feed on human food and are not known to spread disease. The larvae are the workers: they eat organic waste very quickly.

Why rear them?

50–80%typical reduction of waste volume
up to ~42%crude protein in dried larvae
Frassnutrient-rich organic fertilizer
Less methanewaste kept out of landfills

Larvae also change the bacteria in the substrate, which can reduce pathogens such as E. coli and Salmonella. Dried larvae are used as sustainable feed for poultry and fish.

Figures above are typical values from published BSF studies. Your own results depend on the substrate, temperature and cage design, which is exactly what this thesis compares.

The five stages

StageTypical durationWhat happens
Egg3–4 daysLaid in dry crevices near waste, then hatch into tiny larvae.
Larva14–21 daysMain feeding stage. Larvae grow fast and drive all waste conversion.
Prepupaabout 1 weekStop feeding, turn dark and climb out of the substrate on their own (self-harvesting).
Pupaabout 1–2 weeksStay still in a dry, dark spot while the fly forms.
Adult5–8 daysMate in bright light and lay eggs. Adults do not eat.
Warmer, well-fed colonies develop faster. Cool or overfed colonies slow down.

Substrates compared in this study

🍌 Banana peels · Cage 01High in potassium and carbohydrates. A strong energy source that needs careful moisture control.
🧈 Tofu okara · Cage 02Very rich in crude protein. Supports dense prepupal mass but ferments and heats up quickly.
♻️ Mixed food waste · Cage 03The baseline, representing typical municipal and agricultural organic waste.

Feeding tips

  • Feed little and often. Overfeeding causes heat, smell and ammonia.
  • Keep the substrate moist, not waterlogged.
  • Use the same amount and schedule in every cage so the comparison stays fair.

Target conditions

FactorTypical guideHow the system reacts
TemperatureAbout 27–32 °C is comfortable for larvaeAbove your max: fan on, heater off. Below your min: heater on, fan off.
HumidityMoist air, about 60–85 %Below your min: humidifier on. Otherwise off.
LightAdults need bright light to mateAuto mode switches the light by time of day and measured lux.
AmmoniaKeep under 50 ppmWarning and red alert above 50 ppm.
Your own min and max limits are set in Settings. The values above are general guides, so confirm them with your adviser's references.

Three control modes

🤖 AutoThe ESP32 switches the fan, heater, humidifier and light using your limits.
🖐️ ManualYou switch each device from the dashboard buttons or the physical switches. The last action wins.
⛔ OffThe physical mode switch is in the middle. Everything is turned off and dashboard controls are locked.

Tabs

  • Home: live readings, colony health, alerts, controls, cameras and activity log. Tap a camera to enlarge it.
  • AI yield: enter grams of feed, press Run, and the Raspberry Pi returns Random Forest, Decision Tree and KNN predictions.
  • Analytics: trend graphs, safe-range and duty-cycle charts, and CSV export.
  • Settings: set temperature and humidity limits, then press Sync to ESP32.
  • Hardware: WiFi strength, memory, uptime and error messages.
Graph history is saved in this browser only. Export to CSV to keep a permanent copy.

If something looks wrong

ProblemWhat to check
Status shows OfflineNo data for 45 seconds. Check ESP32 power, the MiFi/WiFi signal and the internet connection.
Buttons are lockedThe system is offline, or the physical mode switch is in the Off position.
"DHT11 Sensors Disconnected"Check sensor wiring. The heater is forced off for safety until readings return.
Ammonia above 50 ppmVentilate, remove wet or old waste and reduce feeding.
Prediction says "No reply"The Raspberry Pi program may be off or offline. Start it and try again.
Camera offlineCheck the camera power and address. The page retries every 30 seconds.
