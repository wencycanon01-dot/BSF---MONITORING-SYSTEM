BSF operations manual

What is a black soldier fly?

Hermetia illucens is a harmless insect whose larvae rapidly consume organic waste. Adults have no functional mouthparts, do not bite and do not spread disease.

Life cycle

Egg, larva, prepupa, pupa, adult. The larval stage lasts about 14 to 21 days and does the bioconversion. Prepupae leave the substrate on their own to pupate, which makes harvesting easy.

Why it matters

Diverting waste from landfills cuts methane, and the larvae turn low-value waste into protein-rich feed (up to about 42% protein) and frass fertilizer. They also reduce E. coli and Salmonella in the substrate.

This study

The system watches the microclimate and compares three substrates: banana peels (Cage 01), tofu okara (Cage 02) and mixed food waste (Cage 03).
